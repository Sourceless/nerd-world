# The World

## How Nerd World Was Made

The world of Nerd World was created using the **[Rice and Dice method](https://thecozyartteacher.com/how-to-make-a-rice-fantasy-map/)**: a handful of uncooked rice grains were scattered across a blank sheet of paper. Where the grains fell became the seeds of geography — mountains, islands, lakes, forests, and settlements emerged from their random arrangement. Dice rolls filled in the details.

This means the world has a genuine randomness to it. No single person designed it. It grew from chance and imagination in equal measure.

The world is still being discovered. Every session pushes the frontier a little further. The map we started with is just the beginning — new locations, factions, and features should be added as play reveals them. If something doesn't fit or needs to change, it can change. The world is alive.

## Geography

The map is still being drawn as adventurers explore. What the history tells us so far:

- **The main island** is the largest landmass. At its centre, between mountains and lakes, stands Caerdal (now spelled Kierdell), the first city.
- **The Caldera** is a vast crater, said to be the body of a dragon that exploded after swallowing a witch who rained fire from the sky.
- **The Lonely Mountain** is home to the chaos dragons. The last of them set its peak on fire.
- **The Golden Sands** is a desert, home to nomads and traders, and the site of the largest known pool of wild magic.
- **The Orail lands** produce surplus agriculture, which they trade with Caerdal (Kierdell).
- **A small island in a lake** holds a portal to the Feywild.
- **Smaller islands** were settled from the Sailing Age onwards. Pulo, Goose Island, Issland, and the Pirate Archipelago are among them.

Wild magic pools and portals are scattered across the world. See the [Timeline]({{ '/timeline/' | relative_url }}) for how it all came to be.

## Known Settlements

- **Kierdell (formerly Caerdal)** — The first city, founded between mountains and lakes. The spelling of its name has changed over time. It was once split between worshippers of the gods of water and fire, stood apart from any empire, fortified itself, and taxed the surrounding villages. It is now the capital of the main island, which it united.
- **Pulo** — An island that made contact with the mainland and was promptly colonised by the Orail.
- **Goose Island** — A lawless pirate stronghold. It lost many people resisting the Blood Court's demand for blood.
- **Issland** — Reclusive, cut off from trade, and the one place the Blood Court never collected from. A recent wild magic accident is forcing its people to seek aid.
- **Nympharum** — A Fae land where travellers from the main island exchange knowledge.
- **The Pirate Archipelago** — Home of the reconsolidated pirates, led by Captain Kennet.

## Factions & Powers

- **The Golden Empire (Golden Sands)** — Desert traders. The fire goddess granted them columns of fire, and they traded exclusively with the Fae by sea. The empire has fallen, and mages and priests now compete for power in the vacuum it left.
- **The Fae Empire** — Built around the Feywild portal. It was trapped by the Blood Court's magic circle, then escaped into a world in darkness.
- **The Blood Court** — Rulers from the mountains who rose, perhaps benevolently, during the Darkness and demanded blood from every island and people. The Union of Empires now sanctions them.
- **The Union of Empires** — The empires, pirates included, signed agreements to keep trade and peace stable.
- **The Pirates** — Territorial and quarrelsome, they reconsolidated in the Pirate Archipelago under Captain Kennet. Captain Fairweather, a Fae pirate, once protected trade between the Golden Sands and the Fae.
- **The Cult of Trevor** — A hedonistic cult on the rise. A new potion is sending fairies drunk, dancing, and into its ranks.
- **The Crimson Priests** — Destroyed in the Great Sacrifice.

### Gods & Powers

- **Tharizdun** — Bored, he seeded the world with wild magic, and made more when the major gods petitioned him.
- **The goddess of Mercy** — As more people prayed to her, the darkness began to subside.
- **The fire goddess** — She granted the Golden Empire its columns of fire.
- **The gods of water and fire** — Their worshippers once divided Caerdal.
- **The god of discovery** — Temples to this god were raised across coastal towns and villages in the Sailing Age.

Gods can be made by sufficient worship, and the major gods resent being neglected.

## History & Lore

The history of the world, from the first farmers to the present day, was built by the players in a game of Microscope. See the **[full Timeline]({{ '/timeline/' | relative_url }})**.

History is written by the survivors. As sessions are played and reports are posted, this section will fill with further legends, rumours, and hard-won facts.
