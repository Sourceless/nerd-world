Timeline of the World

The history of Nerd World was built together in a game of Microscope. Each player added great periods of history and the events within them, with every one marked as either Light (hopeful, prosperous) or Dark (tragic, violent). Nobody planned it; it emerged from the table.

Treat it like any history: it is what people believe happened. Myths may be wrong, and sessions can uncover what really happened.

Adding to History

The timeline lives in _data/timeline.yml. Periods run oldest to newest; add events under the period they belong to. If a session reveals something new about the past, add it there and let the world grow.
