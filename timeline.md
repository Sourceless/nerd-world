# Timeline of the World

This history was built by the players in a game of **Microscope**, a collaborative worldbuilding game. Each period and event is marked <span class="tone tone-light">Light</span> or <span class="tone tone-dark">Dark</span> depending on whether it was a good or a grim turn for the world.

Treat this as the established backdrop of Nerd World. DMs are free to build on it, but please don't contradict it. Note that Caerdal and Kierdell are the same city, with the spelling changing over time. See [The World]({{ '/world/' | relative_url }}) for more.

<div class="timeline">

<div class="period tone-light">
<h2>Early Civilisation <span class="tone tone-light">Light</span></h2>
<p class="period-desc">The beginnings of agriculture, recorded history, and permanent residence.</p>
<ul>
<li class="tone-light">Origin myth of the Caldera: a witch rained fire from the sky over the sea. A dragon rose up from the water to swallow her and exploded, and his body formed the Caldera.</li>
<li class="tone-light">Tharizdun gets bored and creates pockets of wild magic in the world.</li>
<li class="tone-light">The first city, Caerdal, is founded between mountains and lakes in the centre of the largest landmass.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-dark">
<h2>The Golden Sand Empire <span class="tone tone-dark">Dark</span></h2>
<p class="period-desc">Desert nomads and traders from a vast empire.</p>
<ul>
<li class="tone-light">Magical and mystical creatures are released from wild magic portals to roam the land.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">The largest pool of wild magic is discovered in the desert.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">It is discovered that a god can be created by sufficient worship.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-dark">
<h2>The Division <span class="tone tone-dark">Dark</span></h2>
<p class="period-desc">Cities and empires become divided in worship and form smaller, like-minded towns.</p>
<ul>
<li class="tone-dark">The first chaos dragon descends from the Lonely Mountain.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">The city of Caerdal is divided between worshippers of the gods of water and fire.</li>
<li class="tone-light">Caerdal discovers the value and power of water beyond survival.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-light">
<h2>The Sailing Age <span class="tone tone-light">Light</span></h2>
<p class="period-desc">The development of sailing technology, and the expansion of human settlement onto smaller islands.</p>
<ul>
<li class="tone-dark">Temples to the god of discovery begin construction, especially in coastal towns and villages.</li>
<li class="tone-light">Caerdal becomes a city without an empire, separate from other nations.</li>
<li class="tone-light">The people of the Orail lands begin trading excess agricultural produce with Caerdal.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">The people of Pulo make contact with the mainland and are promptly colonised by the Orail.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-light">
<h2>The Fae Empire <span class="tone tone-light">Light</span></h2>
<p class="period-desc">A wild magic event reveals that a small island in a lake holds a portal to the Feywild. The Fae build an empire.</p>
<ul>
<li class="tone-dark">Caerdal fortifies the city and begins taxing surrounding villages.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">The Golden Empire establishes exclusive sea trade with the Fae Empire.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-dark">
<h2>Pirates Rule the Seas <span class="tone tone-dark">Dark</span></h2>
<ul>
<li class="tone-light">Pirates are very territorial and constantly fighting among themselves.</li>
<li class="tone-light">Nations, towns, and cities start sharing information to develop longer-range ship weaponry to defeat the pirates.</li>
<li class="tone-light">The Fae pirate Captain Fairweather provides protection to trade between the Golden Sands Empire and the Fae Empire.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-dark">
<h2>Issland <span class="tone tone-dark">Dark</span></h2>
<p class="period-desc">Issland becomes reclusive and cuts itself off from trade borders.</p>
<ul>
<li class="tone-light">Pirates retreat to Goose Island and retain it as a lawless stronghold.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-light">
<h2>The Golden Age <span class="tone tone-light">Light</span></h2>
<p class="period-desc">Nothing to do with the Golden Empire specifically; it means prosperity. Having a specific god to worship for everything leads to an increase in blessings.</p>
<ul>
<li class="tone-dark">The major gods get annoyed by the lack of worship.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">The major gods petition Tharizdun for aid, and he makes more wild magic.</li>
<li class="tone-light">Weakened by infighting, the pirates reconsolidate in the Pirate Archipelago, led by Captain Kennet.</li>
<li class="tone-light">Travellers from the main island go to Nympharum to exchange knowledge with the Fae.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-dark">
<h2>The Ash Age <span class="tone tone-dark">Dark</span></h2>
<ul>
<li class="tone-dark">The Blood Court trap the Fae portal with a gigantic magic circle.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-dark">
<h2>The Darkness <span class="tone tone-dark">Dark</span></h2>
<p class="period-desc">The sky went dark and nothing would grow on the land.</p>
<ul>
<li class="tone-dark">Water-based trade halts due to fear of monsters drawn out by the darkness.</li>
<li class="tone-light">The Blood Court rise to power as benevolent(?) rulers from the mountains.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">The Blood Court demand blood from all islands and people. Goose Island loses many people in the ensuing fighting against this rule. On Issland, no collector or envoy makes it back, and no blood is taken from Issland.</li>
<li class="tone-light">Prior to the granting of the fire columns, the darkness is the most peaceful time ever known. Empires live in harmony in order to survive, and islands are isolated.</li>
<li class="tone-light">The Golden Empire is granted columns of fire from the fire goddess.</li>
<li class="tone-light">People pray to the goddess of Mercy. More and more people start to follow, and the darkness starts to subside. Coincidence?</li>
<li>The Fae discover they can use the portal to escape the magic circle, but when they do they find the world plunged in darkness, unbeknownst to them.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-dark">
<h2>The Great Sacrifice <span class="tone tone-dark">Dark</span></h2>
<p class="period-desc">Mages perform a mass ritual to end the darkness.</p>
<ul>
<li class="tone-dark">The fall of the Golden Empire and the destruction of the crimson priests.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">The last chaos dragon sets the peak of the Lonely Mountain on fire.</li>
<li class="tone-light">The Union of Empires sanctions the Blood Court.</li>
<li class="tone-light">The empires, including the pirates, sign agreements to establish stable trade and peace.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="period tone-dark">
<h2>Present Day <span class="tone tone-dark">Dark</span></h2>
<p class="period-desc">Post famine and disease. A disgruntled militia, an unhappy population, and weakened rulers.</p>
<ul>
<li class="tone-dark">The hedonistic cult of Trevor begins to rise.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">A new potion is spreading through the fairies. They act drunk, dance, and join the cult of Trevor.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">A wild magic accident forces the people of Issland to start seeking aid.</li>
<li class="tone-light">Kierdell (the city once spelled Caerdal) becomes the capital city of the main island, uniting the island.</li>
<li class="tone-light">The Day of Light becomes a worldwide day of festivity and celebration.</li>
<li class="tone-dark">The power vacuum in the Golden Sands following the fall of the priesthood leads to political and social competition between mages and other priests.</li>
<li class="tone-light">Using the Fae portal as an example, wild magic locations are stabilised into portals to new planes and planets.</li>
</ul>
</div>
